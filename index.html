Questions

Before you hop in.

Can't find what you need? Our support team is awake in every city, every hour.

Visit help centre
How is my fare calculated?

Your fare is based on distance, time, and current demand. We show the full amount before you book, and that is what you pay. No meter surprises, no cash negotiations.

Can I pay with cash?

Yes. Choose cash when you book, or link a card, UPI, or wallet in the app. You can switch your payment method right up until the trip ends.

What safety checks do drivers go through?

Every driver completes an identity check, a driving licence verification, and a background check before their first trip. Vehicles are inspected at onboarding and rated by riders after every ride.

Can I book a ride for someone else?

Yes. Add a guest rider in the app, and they will receive the driver details and live tracking link. You can follow the trip from your own phone as well.

What happens if my driver is late?

You will see live arrival updates. If pickup takes longer than the promise window, we will credit a small wait voucher to your account automatically.
