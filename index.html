    Book a ride
    How is the price decided?

    You and the rider agree the price by phone before the ride. You can give your budget when you request a ride, or just ask the rider. Don't get in before you have agreed the price.

    How do I pay?

    Pay your rider in cash or by UPI at the end of the trip. There is nothing to pay online.

    How many people fit in one auto?

    Up to 3 adults with small bags. For bigger groups, book a second auto with the same pickup.

    My flight or train is late. What happens?

    Add your flight or train number in the pickup details when you book. We'll keep an eye on it and adjust your pickup.

    Is the driver a temple guide?

    Our drivers know the temples well and are happy to point you around, but they are not official guides. Licensed guides are available at the Western Group entrance.

    How do I change or cancel a booking?

    Tell the rider when they call, or call or WhatsApp them any time before pickup. Keep your request code handy.
